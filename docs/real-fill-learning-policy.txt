TESTSTOCK REAL-FILL LEARNING POLICY

Robinhood Agentic account history is the source of truth for real-money performance. Teststock's modeled trade-history.json may still be used for scanner calibration, but it must never override confirmed Robinhood fills, exits, positions, or realized P&L.

On every Claude autopilot run:
1. Reconcile the persistent Teststock real-trade journal against Robinhood order history, option order history, positions, closed positions, and account/activity history exposed by the connected tools.
2. Create or update one journal record per real Teststock position. Use stable Robinhood order IDs whenever available so the same trade cannot be counted twice.
3. Record actual average entry fill, quantity/contracts, entry time, planned entry, initial stop/invalidation, protective order IDs/status, targets, actual exit fills, exit reason, realized P&L, realized return, estimated slippage, and realized R when reliable.
4. Also record setupType and riskCluster when the Teststock signal supplies enough information.
5. TIMING + GAP TELEMETRY: when reliable, record entrySessionBucket, priorClose, regularSessionOpen, openingGapPct, and gapRiskPctOfPlannedStopDistance. Use OPEN_0_30, MORNING_30_120, MIDDAY, LATE_SESSION, EXTENDED, or UNKNOWN. Never infer a session bucket from an unreliable timestamp.
6. For every confirmed entry fill, calculate adverseEntrySlippagePct versus the planned/expected executable entry when reliable. For exits, record adverseExitSlippagePct when a reliable planned stop/target/limit reference exists. Never invent a reference price.
7. Record execution telemetry only when the runtime actually exposes it: submitted order type, submitted limit/stop price, bid/ask and spread at submission, fill timestamp, time-to-fill, protective-order creation/verification timestamps, and protection latency. Missing telemetry stays UNKNOWN/null.
8. FRACTIONAL PROTECTION TELEMETRY: for every stock position record protectionMode as BROKER, HYBRID, SYNTHETIC, UNPROTECTED, or UNKNOWN. For SYNTHETIC/HYBRID fractional protection also record the saved synthetic stop, last monitor timestamp, observed trigger timestamp/price when triggered, market-sell submission timestamp, actual fill, detection-to-submit latency, and slippage versus the saved invalidation when reliable. Never mark a synthetic stop as broker-verified protection.
9. EXIT POLICY TELEMETRY: record the validated exit policy used for the trade and the runner percentage actually authorized. Never infer that a runner was allowed from price action alone.
10. PERSISTENCE SELF-TEST: use the journal persistence block to write a unique testNonce and timestamp, then verify the SAME nonce can be read on a later scheduled run. Mark persistence PERSISTENT only after a successful cross-run read-back. If unavailable, reconstruct relevant recent statistics directly from Robinhood history and never use missing persistence as a reason to increase risk.
11. A trade is RESOLVED only when Robinhood confirms the position is closed. Ambiguous/partial/unmatched records remain UNKNOWN/OPEN.
12. Never infer a fill from a submitted order. Never infer an exit from price touching a target/stop. Robinhood fill/position history must confirm it.
13. Recompute overall summary statistics from resolved real trades after reconciliation: wins, losses, win rate, total realized P&L, average winner, average loser, average realized R, average adverse entry/exit slippage, average time-to-fill, broker protective-exit verification rate, synthetic-stop execution reliability, synthetic detection-to-submit latency, and observed drawdown when reliable equity history exists.
14. Recompute the same core performance statistics separately by setupType, riskCluster, symbol, protectionMode, entrySessionBucket, and gap bucket. Keep UNKNOWN buckets instead of guessing classifications.
15. EXECUTION-AWARE PRIORITY: among multiple otherwise-qualified Teststock candidates, confirmed Robinhood execution evidence may change priority conservatively. Prefer a candidate with materially lower adverse entry slippage, faster reliable fills, and better protection reliability only when there are at least 5 comparable confirmed observations for the relevant symbol/setup/cluster. Execution evidence may demote, reduce, or block a candidate; it may not make an otherwise-ineligible setup eligible.
16. TIME-OF-DAY LEARNING: only after at least 10 resolved real trades in an entrySessionBucket may that bucket be automatically reduced or blocked for weak realized performance. A strong time bucket may improve priority among otherwise-equal candidates but may never increase entry size.
17. GAP LEARNING: only after at least 10 resolved real trades in a comparable opening-gap bucket may that bucket be automatically reduced or blocked. Existing hard gap limits remain ceilings and can never be loosened by good historical results.
18. POST-TRADE ATTRIBUTION: after a trade resolves, record any supported attribution categories from SELECTION, ENTRY_TIMING, GAP, SPREAD_SLIPPAGE, STOP_PLACEMENT, EXIT_POLICY, RUNNER, REGIME, PROTECTION_FAILURE, or UNKNOWN, together with concrete evidence. Multiple categories may apply. Prefer UNKNOWN over guessing causality.
19. MODEL/SHADOW LOCK: model history, scanner simulations, shadow trades, backtests, and challenger strategies may rank research candidates and may reduce risk, but they may never increase live allocation above the base Teststock allocation. Only confirmed Robinhood evidence can participate in future performance scaling, and even then every capital-ladder and risk ceiling still applies.
20. DAILY SUMMARY: once per calendar day, store one concise daily summary containing account value, cash buying power, open positions, open planned risk when reliable, realized P&L, trades today, current regime, blocked setup/time/gap buckets, system health, journal-persistence status, exit-policy champion, correlation exposure, and protectionMode for each open stock.

REAL-FILL ADAPTATION — CONSERVATIVE ONLY
- Fewer than 10 resolved real trades total: do not increase risk based on the real-fill sample. Existing Teststock limits remain the maximum.
- At least 10 resolved real trades and average realized R <= 0: reduce all new-entry size by 50% until average realized R is positive again over at least 10 resolved trades.
- At least 10 resolved real trades and win rate < 40%: no new options and reduce stock/crypto size by 50%.
- At least 15 resolved real trades and average realized R < -0.25: pause all new positions; manage exits only until a later reconciled sample improves.
- For a setupType or riskCluster with at least 5 resolved trades, if average realized R <= -0.25 or win rate < 35%, block new entries from that exact bucket until it improves or the user reviews it.
- For a setupType or riskCluster with at least 5 resolved trades and average realized R <= 0, reduce new-entry size for that exact bucket by 50%.
- For an entrySessionBucket or comparable opening-gap bucket with at least 10 resolved real trades and average realized R <= 0, reduce new-entry size in that exact bucket by 50%; if average realized R <= -0.25 or win rate <35%, block the bucket until reviewed/improved.
- LIVE SLIPPAGE GUARD: with at least 5 confirmed entries in the exact bucket, if average adverse entry slippage exceeds 0.25% for STOCK, 5% for OPTION, or 0.40% for CRYPTO, reduce new-entry size in that bucket by 50%. Higher repeated slippage may block that bucket.
- BROKER PROTECTION RELIABILITY GUARD: if at least 5 recent whole-share stock/option entries that should support persistent protection exist and verified broker protection succeeded on fewer than 90%, pause new entries requiring broker-resident protection until reliability is restored.
- FRACTIONAL SYNTHETIC PROTECTION GUARD: synthetic fractional protection is a degraded fallback, not equivalent to broker protection. New fractional stock entries require the current signal's smaller synthetic-risk cap and verified active monitoring interval. If monitoring cannot be verified, new fractional stock entries are blocked. If at least 5 synthetic-stop trigger events exist and more than 1 failed to submit promptly, or average detection-to-submit latency exceeds the encoded monitoring interval, block new fractional stock entries until the monitoring path is repaired and reviewed.
- If any synthetic-only fractional position is currently UNPROTECTED because monitoring failed, open no additional fractional stock exposure and do not keep a fractional runner unattended.
- Per-symbol learning may reduce or block size only when there are at least 5 resolved real trades or confirmed entry fills for that exact symbol. Never increase size from a sample smaller than 10 for the exact symbol/setup/cluster bucket.
- Good performance may never override the capital ladder, profit locks, account floor, circuit breakers, spread/slippage limits, funding lock, event blackouts, holdout-regime guard, portfolio-correlation guard, critical-dependency health guard, fractional monitoring gate, or any smaller risk limit.
- The real-fill journal may make Teststock more conservative automatically. It must not make the system more aggressive than the current Teststock signal without a future explicit policy change.

The persistent journal should use the schema at:
https://sablewolphen.github.io/Teststock/teststock-real-trade-journal-template.json

If a persistent project/workspace journal file is unavailable on a run, reconstruct the relevant recent real-trade statistics directly from Robinhood history before considering a new trade. Do not guess and do not use missing history to justify more risk.
