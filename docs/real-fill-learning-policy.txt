TESTSTOCK REAL-FILL LEARNING POLICY

Robinhood Agentic account history is the source of truth for real-money performance. Teststock's modeled trade-history.json may still be used for scanner calibration, but it must never override confirmed Robinhood fills, exits, positions, or realized P&L.

On every Claude autopilot run:
1. Reconcile the persistent Teststock real-trade journal against Robinhood order history, option order history, positions, closed positions, and account/activity history exposed by the connected tools.
2. Create or update one journal record per real Teststock position. Use stable Robinhood order IDs whenever available so the same trade cannot be counted twice.
3. Record actual average entry fill, quantity/contracts, entry time, planned entry, initial stop/invalidation, protective order IDs/status, targets, actual exit fills, exit reason, realized P&L, realized return, estimated slippage, and realized R when the inputs are reliable.
4. A trade is RESOLVED only when Robinhood confirms the position is closed. Ambiguous/partial/unmatched records remain UNKNOWN/OPEN and are excluded from any rule that could increase risk.
5. Never infer a fill from a submitted order. Never infer an exit from price touching a target/stop. Robinhood fill/position history must confirm it.
6. Recompute summary statistics from resolved real trades after reconciliation: wins, losses, win rate, total realized P&L, average winner, average loser, average realized R, and observed drawdown when reliable equity history exists.

REAL-FILL ADAPTATION — CONSERVATIVE ONLY
- Fewer than 10 resolved real trades total: do not increase risk based on the real-fill sample. Existing Teststock limits remain the maximum.
- At least 10 resolved real trades and average realized R <= 0: reduce all new-entry size by 50% until average realized R is positive again over at least 10 resolved trades.
- At least 10 resolved real trades and win rate < 40%: no new options and reduce stock/crypto size by 50%.
- At least 15 resolved real trades and average realized R < -0.25: pause all new positions; manage exits only until a user review or until a later reconciled sample of at least 15 resolved trades improves above -0.10 R.
- Per-symbol or per-setup learning may reduce or block size only when there are at least 5 resolved real trades for that exact symbol/setup. Never increase size from a sample smaller than 10 for the exact bucket.
- Good performance may never override the capital ladder, profit locks, account floor, daily/weekly circuit breakers, spread/slippage limits, funding lock, or any smaller risk limit.
- The real-fill journal may make Teststock more conservative automatically. It must not make the system more aggressive than the current Teststock signal without a future explicit policy change.

The persistent journal should use the schema at:
https://sablewolphen.github.io/Teststock/teststock-real-trade-journal-template.json

If a persistent project/workspace journal file is unavailable on a run, reconstruct the relevant recent real-trade statistics directly from Robinhood history before considering a new trade. Do not guess and do not use missing history to justify more risk.
