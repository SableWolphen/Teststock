TESTSTOCK REAL-FILL LEARNING POLICY — ADAPTIVE ENGINE V1

SOURCE OF TRUTH
- Robinhood-confirmed real fills, exits, positions, and realized P&L are authoritative for real-money learning.
- The persistent machine journal is docs/data/real-trade-journal.json.
- The generated learning state is docs/data/adaptive-performance.json.
- Shadow/model history remains separate and diagnostic only. It must never be merged into real-money performance statistics.
- Never infer fills, exits, prices, quantities, slippage, or protection from market-price touches. Missing fields remain UNKNOWN/null.

REAL-TRADE INGESTION
- A trade may affect adaptive live ranking only when reconciledFromRobinhood=true, the stock position is confirmed closed, outcome is WIN/LOSS/FLAT, and realizedR is finite.
- Record stable order identifiers when available, symbol, setupType, runtimeRegime, riskCluster, entrySessionBucket, signal generation, planned entry/stop/targets, actual average entry/exit, realized P&L, realized R, spread/slippage when observable, protection mode, and exit reason.
- Update an existing journal trade by stable identity rather than duplicating it.
- Claude/Robinhood remains the source of broker facts. If the execution environment cannot persist directly to GitHub, it must not claim that learning history was saved; instead emit a structured confirmed-fill record for ingestion into the journal by a GitHub-capable Teststock session.

ADAPTIVE ENGINE
- scripts/build-adaptive-performance.mjs rebuilds adaptive-performance.json from reconciled real stock trades on every Teststock scan.
- Performance is summarized overall and by market regime, setup type, regime + setup type, risk cluster, entry-session bucket, and symbol only at a higher sample threshold.
- Recent outcomes are weighted with a 60-day half-life while long-term history remains present.
- Small samples are shrunk toward neutral using 12 pseudo-samples so short winning/losing streaks cannot dominate ranking.
- Minimum 12 resolved real trades in a bucket are required before automatic priority changes.
- Minimum 20 resolved real trades are required before a weak bucket may be automatically temporarily blocked.
- Symbol-specific learning requires at least 20 resolved real trades for that symbol.

WHAT LEARNING MAY DO
- Raise or lower tournament priority among candidates that already pass every ordinary Teststock eligibility gate.
- Reduce candidate size below its ordinary allowed size.
- Temporarily block a sufficiently sampled weak regime/setup/cluster/session/symbol pattern.
- Prefer historically stronger already-qualified patterns when evidence is meaningful.

WHAT LEARNING MAY NEVER DO
- Create eligibility for a setup that failed a base Teststock gate.
- Increase maximum live allocation, planned stop risk, leverage, deployed-capital ceiling, or trade-frequency ceiling.
- Loosen the account floor, capital ladder, circuit breakers, spread/gap rules, holdout validation, blocked-regime policy, correlation guard, event guard, protection requirements, no-margin rule, no-averaging-down rule, or no-widened-stop rule.
- Tune against untouched validation/holdout datasets.
- Treat shadow results as real-money evidence.

TOURNAMENT USE
- scripts/apply-adaptive-tournament-ranking.mjs applies adaptive evidence after the ordinary Stock Tournament is built.
- A-tier still has priority over B-tier. Within the eligible tier, adaptive score may reorder already-qualified candidates.
- A real-fill TEMP_BLOCK can only reduce/remove a candidate; it cannot promote another candidate that otherwise failed Teststock gates.
- Robinhood live price, spread, account, position/order, protection, and user-approval checks remain required after adaptive ranking.

REPORTING
When the adaptive state materially changes, summarize examples in this style:
TRENDING_UP + BREAKOUT: 18 trades, shrunk 58% wins, +0.31R -> PRIORITY_UP
CALM + PULLBACK: 24 trades, shrunk 40% wins, -0.29R -> TEMP_BLOCK

The adaptive layer exists to learn conservatively from actual trading. Good results may improve priority, but they never grant permission to take more maximum risk than the base Teststock system already allows.
