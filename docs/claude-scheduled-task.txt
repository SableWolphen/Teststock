TESTSTOCK AUTOPILOT — SCHEDULED CLAUDE TASK

Run this task once per hour.

1. Read https://sablewolphen.github.io/Teststock/signal.json and follow its current hardRules, hardAccountFloor, capitalLadder, performanceScalingGuard, probabilityFirstPolicy, profitLock, circuitBreakers, tradeFrequencyGuard, correlationGuard, executionQuality, symbolProbationPolicy, exitAutomation, healthGuard, portfolioGuard, outcomeGuard, assetClassRouting, entries, stops, and targets exactly.
2. Read https://sablewolphen.github.io/Teststock/real-fill-learning-policy.txt and apply it before any new entry. Maintain a persistent file named Teststock-Real-Trades.json in this Cowork project/task workspace using https://sablewolphen.github.io/Teststock/teststock-real-trade-journal-template.json as its schema. Robinhood history is authoritative.
3. Reconcile the journal against Robinhood orders, fills, positions, closed positions, and available history before any new risk. Never infer fills. Unknown/partial records stay UNKNOWN/OPEN and can never justify more risk.
4. Verify journal persistence across runs with persistence.testNonce. If persistence fails, reconstruct relevant recent statistics from Robinhood history and never use missing persistence to increase risk.
5. Recompute real-fill performance overall and by setupType, riskCluster, and symbol. Weak realized R, win rate, slippage, or protection reliability may reduce size or block a bucket. Learning may only make risk more conservative.
6. FUNDING LOCK: use only money already inside the dedicated Robinhood Agentic account. Never initiate deposits, ACH, card transfers, wires, bank transfers, internal transfers, withdrawals, or any other money movement. The account may currently contain about $100; profits may be reused and later user-added money may be used, but only the user may fund it.
7. Read live Agentic-account equity, NON-MARGIN CASH BUYING POWER, positions, open orders, status, and reliable history.
8. MARGIN / BORROWED MONEY: DO NOT USE IT. The user is willing to consider margin only if it cannot lose money, and no real market margin position can satisfy that condition. Therefore margin remains disabled. Never borrow, create a margin debit, use cash advances, or increase buying power with debt. If future rules ever reconsider margin, it requires a separate explicit policy change after broker eligibility, costs, maintenance requirements, and real-money evidence are reviewed; current Teststock does not authorize it.
9. HARD FLOOR: below $75 equity, manage exits only. New-entry eligibility may resume at $80+ only if every other guard passes.
10. Select the exact equity tier: >=0/<80 RECOVERY; >=80/<100 DEFENSE; >=100/<125 PROVE; >=125/<150 SCALE; >=150 GROWTH. Exactly $100 is PROVE.
11. Apply performanceScalingGuard after the equity tier. Equity alone cannot unlock higher risk; missing or weak real-fill evidence caps the effective risk tier.
12. Apply profitLock and count total planned stop-loss across all open positions plus the proposed trade. Never intentionally size below the protected-equity floor.
13. Apply circuit breakers: if reliable history shows daily loss >=3% or rolling 7-day drawdown >=6%, open no new positions and continue protective management.
14. TRADE FREQUENCY: multiple qualified opportunities are allowed. Do not exceed 3 new Teststock positions in one calendar day, 10 new Teststock positions in rolling 7 days, 1 new option position per day, or 1 new crypto position per day. These are maxima, not targets. Never force trades to fill the quota.
15. PORTFOLIO CAP: up to 4 concurrent Teststock positions may be held only when each independently qualifies and total deployed cash, planned stop risk, correlation, event, execution, and protection limits all pass. At a ~$100 account, practical sizing may result in fewer positions.
16. CORRELATION: do not stack the same risk cluster. Existing correlated exposure can make a new entry smaller or ineligible.
17. ENTRY-GATE ROBUSTNESS: inspect signal.entryGateRobustness. Use separate development, 2024-2025 untouched holdout, and 2026+ second untouched holdout evidence. A failed untouched regime blocks/reduces the affected regime. Sparse second-holdout evidence reduces risk; it never justifies more risk.
18. REGIME-SPECIFIC ENTRIES: apply the encoded stricter profile for TRENDING_UP, CALM, VOLATILE, TRENDING_DOWN, MIXED, or UNKNOWN. Never substitute one regime's easier conditions for another.
19. BREADTH: require the encoded breadth confirmation. Broad participation strengthens a candidate; weak breadth blocks/reduces it according to the plan.
20. SYMBOL PROBATION: lightly tested symbols start reduced. Historical sample depth and real Robinhood fills may promote them. Weak symbol-level real-fill performance may reduce or block them.
21. GAP RISK: when reliable prior regular-session close and current open exist, skip or wait for revalidation if the opening gap is >4% absolute or consumes >50% of the planned entry-to-stop distance. Never widen a stop to accommodate a gap.
22. LIQUIDITY: prefer tighter spreads, higher reliable dollar volume, and cleaner historical fills. Stock live spread above 0.35%, option above 10%, or crypto above 0.75% blocks a new entry. A missed trade is better than a poor fill.
23. Apply probabilityFirstPolicy after all base gates. Stocks are primary. A stock must satisfy the current minimum historical sample, win-rate, conservative expected-R, reward/risk, quality, volatility, regime, breadth, symbol-probation, event, gap, liquidity, and real-fill restrictions.
24. EVENT BLACKOUT: obey encoded earnings/filing/corporate-action windows. Options use the stricter event window.
25. CRITICAL DEPENDENCY FAIL-CLOSED: before every entry verify signal freshness, dual-holdout/regime validation, probability overlay, live Robinhood balances/positions/orders, journal reconciliation or reliable reconstruction, and required protective-exit capability. Missing/stale/contradictory/unknown critical data means no new position.
26. Keep STOCK, OPTION, and CRYPTO routing separate. Never substitute between classes.
27. If a signal is stale, expired, WAIT, WAIT_FOR_TRIGGER, WAIT_FOR_MARKET_OPEN, DO_NOT_TRADE, DO_NOT_CHASE, or otherwise non-actionable, do nothing for that asset class.
28. STOCK: buy only independently AUTO_BUY_ELIGIBLE orders inside the live entry range. More than one stock may be opened when independently qualified and total portfolio/risk/frequency/correlation rules remain satisfied. Use the smallest applicable sizing cap from all rules.
29. OPTION: stocks/cash remain default. Before any option require the current real-fill evidence unlock plus all elite research gates, holdout/regime/event permission, whole-contract risk fit, acceptable spread/liquidity, exact contract availability, active tier permission, and protective-exit capability. If one whole contract is too large, skip it. Never substitute a lottery contract.
30. CRYPTO: crypto is optional. Require current real-fill evidence unlock plus the encoded A/A+ research gates, BTC context for altcoins, liquidity, risk, and runtime broker support. Only one crypto position may be open at a time.
31. Prefer limit orders where supported. Never pay above maximumEntry. Record observed order/fill/spread/dollar-volume/gap/slippage/protection telemetry; never invent values.
32. A new stock or option is not complete until the strongest supported persistent protective exit is created and verified for the full live quantity. If it cannot be created/verified, do not open the position.
33. Do not assume bracket/OCO support. Verify remaining quantity and conflicting sell orders before partial profits or protective-order replacements.
34. At target1, protect gains and take partial profit when practical; then protect the remainder at breakeven or tighter without widening risk. At target2, prioritize taking profit on most or all remaining size unless a tighter trailing plan is explicitly encoded.
35. Never use margin, borrowed money, naked/undefined-risk options, short stock, leveraged crypto, averaging down, adding to losers, wider stops, duplicate positions, or duplicate open orders.
36. Obey outcomeGuard and real-fill learning. Weak recent results may reduce size, lock options/crypto, or pause new entries.
37. SHADOW DATA is diagnostic only. Never treat shadow results as real P&L or automatic permission to loosen live rules. Do not recommend a shadow-driven gate review before at least 30 accepted and 30 rejected shadow decisions resolve.
38. Re-read buying power/equity/positions after every fill and reconcile the journal after every confirmed entry/exit/cancel/protection change. Material Teststock/Robinhood disagreement means no new risk.
39. DAILY SUMMARY: once per day according to signal.dailySummaryPolicy, report account value, cash, positions, planned risk, realized P&L, trades today, rolling 7-day trades, current regime/breadth, blocked setups, health, journal persistence, equity tier, and effective risk tier.
40. Never increase risk merely to make money faster or recover losses. Maximize expected compounding inside the complete rule set. Cash is valid.

No strategy can guarantee profit or prevent loss. Margin is specifically excluded because the user's condition for margin is no loss, which cannot be guaranteed in real markets.
