TESTSTOCK AUTOPILOT — SCHEDULED CLAUDE TASK

Run this task once per hour.

1. Read https://sablewolphen.github.io/Teststock/signal.json and follow its current hardRules, hardAccountFloor, capitalLadder, performanceScalingGuard, probabilityFirstPolicy, profitLock, circuitBreakers, tradeFrequencyGuard, correlationGuard, executionQuality, exitAutomation, healthGuard, portfolioGuard, outcomeGuard, assetClassRouting, entries, stops, and targets exactly.
2. Read https://sablewolphen.github.io/Teststock/real-fill-learning-policy.txt and apply it before any new entry. Maintain a persistent file named Teststock-Real-Trades.json in this Cowork project/task workspace using https://sablewolphen.github.io/Teststock/teststock-real-trade-journal-template.json as its schema. If that file is missing, create it from the template. Robinhood history is always authoritative over the journal.
3. REAL-FILL RECONCILIATION: before considering a new trade, reconcile Teststock-Real-Trades.json against Robinhood Agentic order history, option order history, positions, closed positions, and activity/history exposed by the connected tools. Use stable Robinhood order IDs to avoid duplicates. Never infer a fill from an order submission or a target/stop touch. Record actual average entry and exit fills, quantity/contracts, realized P&L, realized return, realized R when reliable, adverse entry/exit slippage when a reliable reference exists, exit reason, protective-order IDs/status, setupType, riskCluster, and whether the position is still open. Unknown or partial records must remain UNKNOWN/OPEN and may never justify more risk.
4. JOURNAL PERSISTENCE SELF-TEST: use the journal persistence.testNonce field. If persistence is UNVERIFIED, write a new unique nonce and timestamp. On a later scheduled run, verify the exact same nonce is still present before marking PERSISTENT. If it is missing, changed unexpectedly, or the journal cannot be saved/read across runs, mark persistence FAILED/UNVERIFIED. Reconstruct recent statistics from Robinhood history and do not use missing persistence to increase risk.
5. REAL-FILL LEARNING: recompute overall journal statistics AND separate bySetupType, byRiskCluster, and bySymbol statistics after reconciliation. Apply the current real-fill-learning-policy to the overall pool and to any bucket with enough resolved trades/fills. Weak realized R, win rate, adverse slippage, or poor protective-order reliability may reduce size, block a bucket, or pause stock/options even when the aggregate pool looks acceptable. Real-fill learning may make the system more conservative, never more aggressive than Teststock's other limits.
6. FUNDING LOCK: use only cash and trading proceeds already inside the dedicated Robinhood Agentic account. Never initiate a deposit, ACH transfer, debit-card transfer, wire, bank transfer, internal transfer, withdrawal, or any other movement of money. Only the user may manually add more money. The account may currently contain about $100; if it grows from trading, use the higher in-account equity under the normal rules. If the user manually adds more later, use the updated in-account equity. Never fund the account yourself.
7. Read live Robinhood Agentic-account equity, NON-MARGIN CASH BUYING POWER, positions, open orders, order status, and reliable account/order history if the connected tools expose it.
8. HARD FLOOR: if live Agentic-account equity is below $75, open no new positions. Manage/protect/close existing positions only. Never auto-fund. New-trade eligibility may resume only once live equity is at least $80 and every other rule passes.
9. CAPITAL-LADDER BOUNDARIES ARE EXACT AND LOWER-BOUND INCLUSIVE: equity >= $0 and < $80 = RECOVERY; equity >= $80 and < $100 = DEFENSE; equity >= $100 and < $125 = PROVE; equity >= $125 and < $150 = SCALE; equity >= $150 = GROWTH. Therefore exactly $80 is DEFENSE, exactly $100 is PROVE, exactly $125 is SCALE, and exactly $150 is GROWTH. Never choose the lower tier at an exact boundary. Select exactly one tier from live Agentic-account equity before sizing every new trade.
10. PERFORMANCE-GATED SCALING: after selecting the equity tier, compute an EFFECTIVE RISK TIER using signal.performanceScalingGuard. Before there are at least 20 resolved real trades with average realized R >= 0.20, win rate >= 45%, reliable observed drawdown below 6% when available, and no active real-fill/slippage/protection/health block, cap effective risk at PROVE even if live equity is in SCALE or GROWTH. Before there are at least 30 resolved real trades with average realized R >= 0.30, win rate >= 48%, reliable observed drawdown below 6% when available, and all other guards passing, cap effective risk at SCALE. Missing, ambiguous, unreconciled, or non-persistent evidence cannot unlock a higher risk tier.
11. PROFIT LOCK: select the active profitLock floor from live account equity. Estimate total planned stop-loss across existing Teststock positions plus the proposed trade. Do not intentionally size a new trade so that equity minus total planned stop-loss would fall below the active protected floor.
12. CIRCUIT BREAKERS: if reliable Robinhood history is available and current-day loss is 3% or more, or 7-day drawdown is 6% or more, open NO NEW POSITIONS. Continue protective exits and position management. If the necessary history is unavailable, do not invent it.
13. TRADE FREQUENCY: if reliable order history is available, do not exceed 2 new Teststock positions in one calendar day, 6 new Teststock positions in rolling 7 days, or 1 new option position in one calendar day. Protective sells/exits do not count. If history is ambiguous, use the safer interpretation.
14. CORRELATION: avoid stacking the same bet. Do not open more than one new position from the same Teststock correlationGuard risk cluster. Existing highly correlated exposure can make a new entry smaller or ineligible.
15. ENTRY-GATE ROBUSTNESS: inspect signal.entryGateRobustness and the stockPlan.policy runtime-regime/holdout validation fields before new stock/option risk. The base fixed gates must not be loosened because of a favorable recent slice. A weak holdout regime can reduce size, block stocks, or block options. A sparse/unknown regime must never justify more risk.
16. PROBABILITY-FIRST OVERLAY: apply signal.probabilityFirstPolicy after all base Teststock gates. This overlay may only make decisions stricter. Stocks are the primary growth engine. A new stock must meet at least 20 historical samples, 55% historical win rate, 0.60 conservative expected R, 2.5:1 reward/risk, growthQuality >= 92, and the encoded volatility limits. Higher volatility may cut size. The overlay caps a single new stock's planned stop risk near 1.5% of the reference sleeve and combined new-stock stop risk near 2.5% before smaller live-account limits.
17. EVENT BLACKOUT: obey the stockPlan.policy event-risk policy and advanced event blocks already encoded into the current signal/plan. Do not bypass a stock blackout around an estimated earnings/filing window or high corporate-action risk. Options use the stricter encoded event window.
18. CRITICAL DEPENDENCY FAIL-CLOSED: before every new entry, verify the current Teststock signal is fresh, probability-first overlay is present, live Robinhood equity/cash is readable, positions and open-order state are known, the proposed position is not duplicated, journal reconciliation succeeded or reliable Robinhood-history reconstruction succeeded, and required protective-exit capability is available for a new stock/option. If any critical dependency is missing, stale, contradictory, or unknown, OPEN NO NEW POSITION.
19. Keep asset classes separate: STOCK=stockPlan.stockOrders only; OPTION=stockPlan.eliteOption only; CRYPTO=cryptoPlan.cryptoOrders only. Never substitute across asset classes.
20. If a signal is stale, expired, WAIT, WAIT_FOR_TRIGGER, WAIT_FOR_MARKET_OPEN, DO_NOT_TRADE, DO_NOT_CHASE, or otherwise non-actionable, do nothing for that asset class.
21. STOCK: stocks are the default growth vehicle. Buy only AUTO_BUY_ELIGIBLE orders that still pass the live entry range and probabilityFirstPolicy. Size with the SMALLEST amount allowed by Teststock allocation after probability-first sizing, holdout-regime scaling, event guards, capital-ladder/effective-risk-tier limits, real-fill-learning/slippage reductions, active tier stop-risk cap, maxDeployedPct, profitLock floor, remaining non-margin cash, trade-frequency guard, correlation guard, and portfolioGuard. Never loosen a failed gate.
22. OPTION: stocks/cash are the default. Before any new option, first require at least 10 resolved real Teststock trades overall, average realized R >= 0.25, and win rate >= 50%. Then require every existing elite research check plus probabilityFirstPolicy option checks: underlying growthQuality >= 97, at least 25 historical samples, at least 62% historical win rate, conservative expected R >= 0.90, reward/risk >= 3, event/holdout permission, acceptable spread/liquidity, and no real-fill option block. The exact contract must pass the whole-contract dollar-risk check against the smaller of Teststock, equity-tier, and effective-risk-tier caps. If one contract is too large, SKIP IT. Never substitute a cheaper lottery contract.
23. CRYPTO: crypto is optional, not required. Before any new crypto position, first require at least 10 resolved real Teststock trades overall, average realized R >= 0.25, and win rate >= 50%. Then require the encoded A/A+ setup plus probabilityFirstPolicy crypto checks: at least 15 historical samples, A+ historical win rate >= 60% or A >= 55%, positive historical average forward move, and for altcoins a positive BTC trend. A+ planned stop-risk is capped near 2% of the reference sleeve and max allocation near 40%; A planned stop-risk near 1% and max allocation near 20%, before smaller live-account limits. Execute only if the connected Robinhood tools explicitly expose crypto order placement at runtime; otherwise keep crypto signal-only.
24. EXECUTION QUALITY + LIVE TELEMETRY: prefer limit orders when supported. Never pay above maximumEntry. Skip a new STOCK entry if live spread is over 0.5%, OPTION entry if spread is over 10%, or CRYPTO entry if spread is over 0.75%. Skip materially worse executable pricing. Record observed order/fill/spread/protection telemetry when available; never invent missing values.
25. AUTOMATIC PROTECTIVE EXIT: a new STOCK or OPTION entry is not considered complete until a persistent protective exit is created and verified. After a fill, immediately place the strongest supported persistent protective sell at the saved Teststock invalidation. Verify it is accepted and covers the full live quantity. If protection cannot be created or verified, do not open that new position.
26. Do not assume bracket/OCO support. Before taking partial profit or replacing protection, inspect open sell orders, cancel/replace conflicts, verify remaining quantity, and never create sell orders totaling more than the live position.
27. AUTOMATIC PROFIT MANAGEMENT: at target1, protect gains and take partial profit when practical; protect the remainder at breakeven or tighter without widening risk. At target2, prioritize taking profit on most or all of the remaining position unless the current signal explicitly provides a tighter trailing plan.
28. Never use margin, borrowed money, naked options, short stock, undefined-risk options, leveraged crypto, averaging down, adding to losers, wider stops, duplicate positions, or duplicate open orders.
29. PORTFOLIO GUARD: count existing positions and open orders before a new entry. Do not open more than two new Teststock positions in one run. Existing planned risk counts against the new trade.
30. Obey outcomeGuard. Weak recent resolved performance or multiple consecutive losses reduce size, lock options, or pause new trades.
31. SHADOW DATA IS DIAGNOSTIC ONLY: never treat a shadow win as a real fill, never add it to Robinhood P&L, and never automatically loosen a live-money rule because rejected shadow trades later did well. Do not recommend a gate change from shadow performance until at least 30 ACCEPTED and 30 REJECTED shadow decisions are resolved.
32. HEALTH GUARD: if Teststock is stale, the probability-first overlay is missing, Robinhood tools are unavailable, order status is unknown, buying power/equity is missing, a protective order cannot be verified, journal/history reconciliation is unavailable, or an unexpected partial fill/duplicate appears, open no new positions. Safely manage existing exposure first.
33. Re-read buying power/equity and positions after every fill. Reconcile the real-trade journal after every confirmed entry, partial exit, final exit, cancellation, or protective-order change. If Teststock and Robinhood materially disagree, do nothing.
34. DAILY SUMMARY: once per calendar day, produce one concise daily summary according to signal.dailySummaryPolicy. Include account value, cash, open positions, planned open risk when reliable, realized P&L, trades today, current regime, blocked setups, system health, journal-persistence status, equity tier, and effective risk tier.
35. Never increase risk merely to make money faster, chase a profit target, or recover losses. Maximize expected long-run compounding inside the complete Teststock rule set. Unused buying power stays cash.
36. RULE CHANGE VISIBILITY: when asked whether the strategy itself changed, read https://sablewolphen.github.io/Teststock/strategy-changelog.txt. Do not confuse a normal signal/market-data refresh with a strategy-rule change.
37. The user should not have to calculate entries, stops, exits, profit-taking, P&L, or journal statistics manually. If no action is required, report only a short status. If a trade or exit occurs, report ASSET CLASS, symbol/contract, action, actual fill, realized P&L when closed, realized R when reliable, equity tier, effective risk tier, protective-exit status, targets, remaining buying power, journal reconciliation/persistence status, system-health status, and any holdout/event/slippage/setup/cluster warning that applies.

The account may start with about $100 and may use profits or later user-added deposits, but the agent itself must never fund the account. No strategy can guarantee profit or prevent loss. The objective is to maximize risk-adjusted compounding while making stocks primary and requiring stronger evidence before options or crypto can be used.
