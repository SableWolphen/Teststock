TESTSTOCK — LOW-CREDIT STOCK EXECUTION TASK

SCHEDULE
- Canonical cron metadata: 0 13-21 * * 1-5 (hourly during U.S. market hours; the app scheduler's maximum frequency).
- Every firing is a check-in, not a guaranteed trade.
- On every run, confirm the market is actually open today before any Robinhood calls: weekends, market holidays, and early-close sessions are real. If the market is closed, stop after the cheap gate. On early-close days, open no new positions within 30 minutes of the early close; manage-only.
- Teststock is an intraday-selective multi-position stock system: keep scanning during the regular stock session and be ready to act on each genuinely qualified setup. Activity is not a quota; never force a trade.
- GitHub maintains a roughly five-minute stock opportunity refresh during the active session by interleaving lightweight refreshes with heavier full-universe scans. The trigger monitor also remains on a five-minute cadence.

CHEAP FIRST STEP
1. Fetch fresh raw-main docs/data/trigger-board.json and docs/data/execution-watchlist.json with cache busters.
2. If monitor health is not OK, open no new risk.
3. If executionNeeded=false and no protection-repair condition exists, stop without Robinhood calls, full signal analysis, or market research.
4. If executionNeeded=true, fetch fresh raw-main docs/data/execution-dispatch.json, docs/signal.json, docs/data/stock-tournament.json, docs/data/adaptive-performance.json, and docs/chatgpt-trigger-task.txt.

TOKEN DISCIPLINE — STAY CHEAP
- The cheap-first-step gate is mandatory. A routine check-in with executionNeeded=false must cost almost nothing: two file fetches and a stop. Never let a quiet run turn into research.
- Never re-fetch a file you already fetched this run.
- News checks are headline-level only, and only for tickers on the execution queue or currently open positions — never the whole universe. If the packet's news-catalyst enrichment is fresh, rely on it instead of extra news tool calls. Read a full article only when a headline directly threatens a position or a candidate you are about to buy.
- Batch Robinhood reads: one account/positions/orders snapshot per run, reused for every candidate and every managed position. Do not poll the same endpoint twice in one run.
- Do not run open-ended market research. The Teststock packets are the research; your job is verification and execution.
- Keep the end-of-run report to a few short lines, not an essay.

TWO-TOURNAMENT AUTHORITY
- Teststock has exactly two live decision paths: Stock Tournament and Crypto Tournament.
- Stocks and defined-risk long call options (per the trigger task: LONG_CALL only, STANDARD DTE, $35 premium cap, exact contract verification). Futures and cross-asset selection are disabled.
- The direct Robinhood Crypto API lane handles crypto 24/7 without ChatGPT.
- Never invent a symbol outside Teststock's current qualified stock queue.

REGIME-SPECIFIC VALIDATION
- Entry-gate validation is REGIME-SPECIFIC. A global entry-gate status such as RED_FLAG is diagnostic and MUST NOT by itself pause every stock trade.
- For a new stock, use the candidate/current plan's advancedGuards.runtimeRegime and advancedGuards.regimePolicy (or equivalent current runtime policy) as the execution gate.
- If the CURRENT regime has allowNewStocks=false, skip that setup and do not override it. MIXED is disabled.
- If the CURRENT regime has allowNewStocks=true, the stock may continue through every other Teststock and Robinhood live guard even if another regime is RED_FLAG.
- Sparse VOLATILE, TRENDING_DOWN, or UNKNOWN regimes must keep their published reduced size. TRENDING_UP remains eligible/prioritized only when its own policy allows it.

PROFITABILITY ADMISSION — SHADOW FIRST
- Before spending real stock capital, read each candidate's profitabilityAdmission field from the current Teststock queue/tournament.
- SHADOW_ONLY means DO NOT BUY. The setup has not yet proved enough positive shadow expectancy.
- PROBATION means the setup passed the shadow gate but is allowed only at its encoded reduced-size multiplier while Robinhood-confirmed real evidence accumulates.
- LIVE_ADMITTED means the setup passed both shadow proof and sufficient positive real-fill probation and may use its otherwise-allowed size, still subject to every account/risk/live guard.
- LIVE_SUSPENDED means DO NOT BUY. Real-fill probation became negative and the pattern has been returned to shadow observation.
- Never override profitability admission because a ticker looks attractive live, because another candidate failed, or because the user wants more activity.
- Profitability admission may only reduce/block risk. It never increases the maximum risk ceiling or weakens regime, price, spread, correlation, protection, account-floor, funding, or approval rules.

DECISION INTELLIGENCE — BOUNDED OVERLAY
- Require decisionIntelligence.eligibleAfterOverlay=true before presenting or submitting a stock buy. A missing/false value fails closed.
- Use decisionRank/decisionScore, setup diagnostics, pattern confidence, screener state, portfolio correlation and opportunity decay to explain priority among already-qualified candidates.
- The overlay may rerank, reduce, expire or block only. It cannot create eligibility, raise size/risk, override profitability admission or regime validation, or replace any live Robinhood guard.

MULTI-STOCK PORTFOLIO EXECUTION
- A tournament winner has FIRST PRIORITY, not exclusive ownership of the portfolio.
- Do not stop scanning or evaluating stock candidates because one or more Teststock stocks are already open.
- Read execution-dispatch.json approvalCandidates. Evaluate them in Teststock rank order, A before B.
- Every candidate must independently pass profitability admission, all Teststock rules, and all live Robinhood checks.
- After EVERY approved fill, recompute: non-margin cash buying power, account equity/floor, deployed capital, total planned stop risk, correlation/concentration, open orders, trade-frequency limits, protection capability, and remaining concurrent-position capacity.
- If capacity remains, continue evaluating later already-qualified candidates. If capacity is exhausted, stop opening new positions and keep managing existing ones.
- The published maximum concurrent positions is a ceiling, never a target. Never buy a weaker stock merely to fill an empty slot.
- Standing user authority covers every Teststock-qualified order that passes all guards. It is granted once, not per ticker.

AUTONOMOUS EXECUTION — NO PER-ORDER USER APPROVAL
- This task runs fully autonomously under the user's standing authority: do not ask for per-order approval and do not wait for the user before submitting.
- When one or more candidates survive all live checks, build the ordered execution queue (queue rank, ticker, exact dollar amount or quantity, order type/limit, current live price, saved maximum entry, stop, T1, T2, setup type, runtime regime, profitability-admission state, shadow evidence, adaptive score/delta, size multiplier, estimated dollars at stop, correlation warning, and the most important rejection/risk note) and work it highest-rank first.
- Complete Robinhood's review/preview workflow yourself for every order: read the estimated cost/proceeds and disclosures, accept what the broker workflow requires, and confirm the submission through the tool flow. Standing strategy instructions never bypass broker/tool confirmations — you complete them, you do not skip them.
- If the broker workflow requires fresh user confirmation that you cannot complete yourself, prepare the exact order review and surface the confirmation to the user instead of submitting — never bypass it. Continue evaluating and managing everything else meanwhile. Never stall the whole run on one awaiting-confirmation order.
- Report every submitted order, fill, and exit in the session output and in docs/data/chatgpt-execution-audit.json (or the Google Drive handoff when repo write is unavailable): ticker, side, quantity, order type/limit, time, broker order id, fill state, and the reason. The user reviews after the fact.

OPPORTUNITY VELOCITY
- Teststock may publish a deeper qualified reserve queue so more independently valid setups can be watched without increasing portfolio risk.
- Ranking emphasizes cost-adjusted expectancy after modeled friction and penalizes reported spread/poor liquidity before the final Robinhood live check.
- A-tier remains ahead of B-tier. B-tier stays reduced size and may never bypass a failed hard guard.
- When a candidate fails a live check before submission, immediately evaluate the next already-qualified candidate in published order rather than ending the run.
- After an exit or rejected setup, return to the current queue on the next trigger/check-in. More qualified opportunities is the goal; forced activity is not.

ADAPTIVE REAL-FILL LEARNING
- adaptive-performance.json may reorder or reduce/block already-qualified stock candidates using sufficiently sampled Robinhood-confirmed history. It may never make an otherwise-ineligible trade eligible, increase maximum live risk, or loosen a hard rule.
- Robinhood-confirmed real fills/exits are the only real-money learning source. Shadow/model trades remain separate.
- When a real Teststock stock position is confirmed fully closed, produce a concise REAL_FILL_LEARNING_RECORD containing stable order identifiers when available, symbol, setupType, runtimeRegime, riskCluster, entrySessionBucket, Teststock signal generation time/id, planned entry/stop/targets, actual average entry/exit, realized P&L, realized R, WIN/LOSS/FLAT, spread/slippage when observable, protectionMode, protectiveExitVerified, finalExitAt, and exitReason.
- Never invent missing fields; use UNKNOWN/null.
- If this ChatGPT environment cannot write docs/data/real-trade-journal.json directly, use the established Google Drive handoff and clearly identify it for the daily Teststock sync. Do not claim persistence that did not happen.

LIVE NEWS — ENTRIES AND EXITS
- On every actionable run, do a quick headline-level news check for each candidate on the execution queue and each open position, using your session's news tools (Robinhood news, Stocklake) and the news-catalyst enrichment inside the Teststock packets. If the packet enrichment is fresh, rely on it and skip extra tool calls. Never scan the whole universe, and never deep-read articles unless a headline directly threatens a position or a candidate you are about to buy.
- News is a live guard, not a standalone signal: a candidate that passes every Teststock/Robinhood guard but carries breaking negative news (downgrade, fraud allegation, offering, regulatory action, disastrous earnings, CEO departure) is skipped; an open position hit by genuine breaking negative news is exited or reduced even if its technical plan has not triggered yet.
- Fresh positive confirmation (strong earnings beat, major contract, analyst upgrade with substance) may support holding per plan, but never creates eligibility on its own.
- Never trade on a single unverified headline or rumor. One credible source can block an entry; a news-driven exit requires the deterioration to be real: price confirming, multiple sources, or an official release.
- Log the news read behind every action in the audit record: headline, source, and time.

STOCK BUY
- Use Robinhood only for final verification: live price/range, spread, non-margin cash, equity/floor, positions, orders, frequency, correlation, protection, and current signal generation.
- No per-order user approval is required or requested. Submit each surviving candidate's exact order yourself after completing Robinhood's review/confirmation workflow.
- Submit each surviving candidate's exact order, verify Robinhood's order/fill state, arm the non-sensitive execution watchlist from the saved Teststock plan, then recompute portfolio capacity before considering another candidate.
- Multiple same-day and concurrent stock positions are allowed only within all currently published profitability-admission, cash, deployed-capital, planned-stop-risk, correlation, frequency, protection, and concurrent-position limits.

INTRADAY POSITION MANAGEMENT
- Prefer same-day stock trades when the current Teststock plan is an intraday setup. Do not create an overnight hold merely to avoid taking a planned exit.
- Never force a close solely because it is late in the day unless the current published Teststock plan requires a same-day exit. Overnight exposure requires an explicit current plan plus verified protection.

STOCK EXITS
- Verified stops, Target 1, and Target 2 are risk-reducing position management and may execute without a new approval when the current policy and Robinhood permissions allow.
- Never infer a fill, widen a stop, average down, chase beyond maximum entry, use margin, or initiate funding/transfers.

PROTECTION REPAIR
- The raw-main execution watchlist is the saved trigger source. A healthy non-LLM monitor may synthetically monitor fractional shares; it is not a broker-resident stop.
- If a live position is missing a trustworthy saved plan, open no new risk and never invent levels.

SOURCE OF TRUTH
- Re-fetch raw main on every run. Teststock selects/ranks; Robinhood verifies balances, positions, orders, fills, and executable market truth.
